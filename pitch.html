Doorway

Every newcomer deserves an open door.

The problem

Moving to Denmark is easy on paper. Finding your people is not.

You have a CPR number, a bike and a job. Then it's a dark Tuesday in November. Who do you call? Who could you ask for career advice, or who would show you how things work here?

Problem statement

Newcomers to Denmark need a meaningful network of people who help them grow, belong and give back. Today there's no easy way to find those people, or to keep those relationships going.

1

Circles close early

Friendships often form in school and stay tight-knit for life. Warm, but hard to join as an adult.

2

Community is hidden in foreninger

Clubs and associations are where life happens, but they're hard to find and join without the language or an invite.

3

Networking feels transactional

LinkedIn and meetups give you contacts, not relationships. After the event, people drift apart.

Denmark regularly ranks among the hardest countries for internationals to make local friends (InterNations, Expat Insider survey).
